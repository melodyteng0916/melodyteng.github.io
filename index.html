Hi, I'm Melody 👋

I graduated from the University of Toronto (Statistics + Commerce) and moved to the Bay Area in April, basically the second I had my degree in hand. I don't really know how to do things halfway, so I've gone all in since landing here: reading papers I half understand, building things on weekends, and asking founders far too many questions.

I'm data oriented at heart. I started out as a data analyst, where I learned to trust the numbers before the narrative. Since moving to SF I've gotten much closer to the product side, working with founders and engineers on what gets built and why.

Outside of work, I host startup community events for founders and builders around the Bay. My favorite part is when a room of strangers turns into people still trading ideas an hour after the event was supposed to end.

I love meeting people and building relationships that outlast any one job or project, so feel free to book a coffee chat with me :)
